# Budget Meal Planning Guide

## Cost-Saving Strategies

1. **Buy in bulk**: Legumes, rice, oats, and frozen vegetables are cheaper in bulk
2. **Use frozen vegetables**: More affordable than fresh and longer shelf life
3. **Seasonal produce**: Buy fruits and vegetables when they're in season
4. **Generic brands**: Often cheaper than name brands with similar quality
5. **Meal prep**: Cook large batches to save time and reduce food waste
6. **Plan around sales**: Check weekly flyers for discounted items

## Tips for Sticking to Budget

1. **Make a shopping list** and stick to it
2. **Avoid impulse buys** - especially pre-packaged convenience foods
3. **Check pantry first** to use what you already have
4. **Cook at home** instead of eating out
5. **Batch cook** grains and legumes to save time and money
6. **Use leftovers** for lunches or next day's dinner
