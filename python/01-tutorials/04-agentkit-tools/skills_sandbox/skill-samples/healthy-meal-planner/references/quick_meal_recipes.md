# Quick Vegan Protein Recipes (Under 30 Minutes)

## Tofu Stir-Fry
**Prep time:** 25 minutes
**Protein per serving:** 28g
**Cost per serving:** $4.50

**Ingredients:**
- 200g firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 2 cups mixed stir-fry vegetables (bell peppers, broccoli, carrots)
- 30g soy sauce
- 15g sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 50g cooked brown rice

**Instructions:**
1. Press tofu and cut into cubes
2. Heat sesame oil in a pan, cook tofu until golden
3. Add garlic, ginger, and vegetables, stir-fry 5 minutes
4. Add soy sauce, cook 2 minutes
5. Serve over brown rice

## Lentil and Chickpea Salad
**Prep time:** 20 minutes
**Protein per serving:** 22g
**Cost per serving:** $3.20

**Ingredients:**
- 100g cooked lentils
- 100g chickpeas, rinsed
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 cucumber, diced
- 30g olive oil
- 20g lemon juice
- 1 tsp cumin
- 50g quinoa, cooked

**Instructions:**
1. Combine lentils, chickpeas, tomatoes, cucumber
2. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, and cumin
3. Toss with salad ingredients
4. Serve over quinoa

## Tempeh Wrap
**Prep time:** 15 minutes
**Protein per serving:** 25g
**Cost per serving:** $3.80

**Ingredients:**
- 100g tempeh, sliced
- 2 whole wheat tortillas
- 1/2 avocado, mashed
- 1 cup spinach
- 1/4 cup shredded carrots
- 20g hummus
- 1 tsp paprika

**Instructions:**
1. Pan-fry tempeh with paprika until crispy
2. Warm tortillas
3. Spread hummus and mashed avocado on tortillas
4. Add tempeh, spinach, and carrots
5. Roll and serve

## Chickpea Curry
**Prep time:** 25 minutes
**Protein per serving:** 20g
**Cost per serving:** $3.50

**Ingredients:**
- 200g chickpeas, rinsed
- 1 cup coconut milk (light)
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 100g spinach
- 60g basmati rice, cooked

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion and garlic until soft
2. Add curry powder, cook 1 minute
3. Add chickpeas and coconut milk, simmer 10 minutes
4. Stir in spinach until wilted
5. Serve with rice
